About

FIX: A world where organizations thrive through inclusive excellence, empowered people, and sustainable impact—transforming higher education, business, and the nonprofit sector into catalysts for equitable and lasting change.

Vision

To be a transformative force in professional development, where diverse perspectives converge to create sustainable, empowered communities. We envision a world where learning never ceases and every effort contributes to a brighter, more equitable future.

Mission

We partner with higher education institutions, businesses, and nonprofits to build organizational capacity, develop inclusive leaders, and create sustainable systems of excellence. Through evidence-based consulting, coaching, and professional development, we empower organizations to align their values with action, maximize their impact, and navigate change with intentionality and care.

Core values

Our work is grounded in values that translate into everyday practices and measurable outcomes.

Intentionality

Clarity that drives execution

We bring clarity of purpose to every engagement, helping organizations define their desired impact and chart deliberate paths forward. Through strategic planning and focused execution, we transform aspirations into actionable roadmaps.

Inclusive Excellence

Inclusion powers better outcomes

We believe excellence emerges when diverse voices shape solutions. We maximize impact by leveraging existing strengths, resources, and perspectives while intentionally removing barriers to full participation and belonging.

Sustainable Impact

Ambition aligned with reality

We balance ambition with reality, aligning expectations, effort, and outcomes with available resources. Our solutions are designed for long-term viability, ensuring organizations can maintain momentum beyond our engagement.

Empowerment

Capacity that outlasts initiatives

We equip individuals and teams to own their growth and amplify their influence and impact. By cultivating growth mindsets and developing practical tools, we build organizational capacity that outlasts any single initiative.

Innovation

Unconventional thinking, grounded solutions

We challenge convention by bringing together diverse perspectives to generate breakthrough solutions. We help organizations think beyond traditional boundaries while remaining grounded in their unique context and values.

Collaboration

Partnerships that amplify impact

We forge intentional partnerships that accelerate alignment and multiply impact. Through strategic relationship-building and cross-functional engagement, we help organizations break down silos and advance shared missions.

Well-being

Care that sustains excellence

We champion both self-care and collective care, recognizing that sustainable excellence requires resilient people. We model and advocate for practices that honor the whole person and build appreciation across teams.

Continuous Improvement

Learning embedded in action

We embed learning into action through evidence-based decision making. By leveraging qualitative and quantitative data, facilitating goal operationalization, and tailoring solutions to diverse needs, we help organizations identify what's working, adapt what is not, and align systems with values. We transform insights into sustainable practices that evolve with purpose and agility.

Accountability

Transparent metrics, informed decisions

We establish meaningful metrics that tell the truth about progress. Through transparent tracking, reporting, and stakeholder communication, we ensure organizations can demonstrate impact and make informed decisions.

Services

We partner with higher education institutions, businesses, and nonprofits to build capacity, drive sustainable change, and maximize impact through evidence-based, values-aligned solutions.

Professional Development

Invest in your people. We design customized learning experiences that build inclusive leadership, change management expertise, strategic communication skills, and team effectiveness. Our programs combine practical tools with mindset development to create lasting individual and collective growth.

Organizational Development

Transform how you work. From strategic planning to operational optimization, we help organizations align structure, processes, and technology with mission and values. Our services include organizational assessment, digital transformation, and initiatives that drive inclusion, grounded in data-driven insights.

Nonprofit Capacity Building

Strengthen your mission. We provide strategic planning, technology implementation, and resource development services tailored to nonprofit needs. Our approach balances programmatic impact with organizational sustainability, helping you demonstrate results and build long-term capacity.

Executive & Leadership Coaching

Amplify your influence. Through one-on-one executive coaching, leadership development, team coaching, and transformational coaching, we help leaders navigate complexity, inspire teams, and drive meaningful change while maintaining well-being and resilience.

Data & Impact Services

Measure what matters. We design evaluation frameworks, provide data analytics, and build accountability systems that transform information into actionable insights. Our approach ensures you can track progress, demonstrate impact, and make evidence-based decisions with confidence.

Principal Consultant

Dr. Ayodele Thomas, Ph.D.

For more than 30 years, Dr. Ayodele Thomas (she/her) has partnered with higher education institutions and nonprofit organizations to drive transformational change and inclusive operational excellence. She combines deep expertise in change management, data analytics, strategic technology implementation, and inclusion to help leaders navigate complexity and achieve measurable, sustainable results.

Dr. Thomas brings clarity to complexity. She excels at aligning diverse stakeholders around shared vision, building collaborative frameworks that bridge silos, and fostering cultures of inclusion, innovation and accountability. Known for her adaptive leadership style and commitment to inclusion, she helps organizations and individuals not only navigate change but emerge stronger and more resilient.

As Associate Vice Provost and Associate Vice President at Stanford University, Dr. Thomas designed and led enterprise-wide frameworks serving 16,000+ staff across 25+ units. Over two-decades, she managed multi-million-dollar budgets, spearheaded technology solutions that eliminated administrative redundancies, and built scalable systems that enhanced both performance and engagement. Her work consistently translated executive vision into actionable strategies that delivered institution-wide impact.

Dr. Thomas has also served more than a decade in nonprofit leadership as an executive director and board officer, providing strategic direction for a youth STEM initiative. In this role, she oversaw comprehensive operations spanning human resources, financial management, compliance, risk management, grant writing, donor stewardship, stakeholder engagement, curriculum development, and facilities management. She strengthened program infrastructure through advanced technology integration, including the development of a sophisticated longitudinal tracking database that enabled data-driven decision-making and demonstrated measurable community impact. This cross-sector experience gives her unique insight into the operational challenges and opportunities facing mission-driven organizations and teams.

Dr. Thomas holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering (Computer Architecture) from Stanford University and is a Prosci® Certified Change Management Practitioner. She resides in Santa Clara, California.
